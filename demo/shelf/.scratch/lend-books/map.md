# Map: Lend books to friends

## Destination

I lend a book to a friend and see who has it. The app tells me when a loan is late.

## Decisions so far

- 01: Open Library gives enough requests for one cover on each loan card.
- 02: No public link. I lend only to people in my contacts.

## Not yet specified

- **Reminders by email or push.** The app can remind me, my friend or both. The channel is open.

## Out of scope

- Lending to people who are not in my contacts
